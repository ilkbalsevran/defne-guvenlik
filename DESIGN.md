## Brand & Style

This design system serves an established security technology and electronics engineering firm based in Balıkesir / İvrindi, specializing in IP camera networks, commercial intruder alarm systems, satellite infrastructure, and solar panel arrays. 

The aesthetic moves deliberately away from ephemeral Silicon Valley SaaS tropes, avoiding translucent glassmorphism, floating orbs, and generic violet gradients. Instead, it embodies a grounded, architectural, and industrial European engineering posture: high-reliability electronics, structural precision, disciplined grid alignment, and crisp technical data hierarchy.

The visual mood conveys vigilance, rock-solid uptime, and engineering craftsmanship. Interfaces feel like modern security operations consoles and architectural blueprints—utilizing high-contrast navy foundations, precise hairline strokes, crisp architectural neutrals, and laser-focused cyan/teal accents that evoke laser sensors, optical lens reflection, and status telemetries.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the firm’s circular camera-aperture emblem and industrial operations context:

- **Primary (`#132238`)**: Industrial deep slate navy. Conveys authority, rock-solid hardware engineering, and optical precision. Used for primary typography, structural dark sections, toolbars, and primary commanding buttons.
- **Secondary (`#0097B2`)**: The signature aperture teal extracted from the emblem’s right hemisphere. Functions as the sharp signal color for active device nodes, primary call-to-actions, and calibrated data callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#0EA5E9`)**: High-luminance tech cyan. Deployed for optical feedback, highlight markers, focus states, and signal pulses across monitoring dashboards.
- **Neutral (`#F4F6F9`)**: Architectural slate ground. Replaces harsh pure white canvas with an engineered, matte off-white surface that mitigates glare and grounds data cards with tangible weight.
- **Named Colors**:
  - `slate-night` (`#0F1A2C`): Extreme low-light surface for camera wall grids and telemetry headers.
  - `border-hairline` (`#E2E8F0`): Ultra-crisp 1px boundary line separating dense engineering specs.
  - `border-dark` (`#1E304B`): Structural hairline divider across dark operations consoles.
  - Status indicators (`#10B981`, `#EF4444`, `#F59E0B`): Strict functional indicators for live streaming, alarm trigger, and system calibration.

## Typography

The typography pairs the geometric authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and brand titles with the relentless clarity and neutral metric legibility of **Inter** for technical body copy, spec sheets, and diagnostic data.

Headlines adopt tightly tracked letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy geometric weights (700, 800) to project structural security and architectural permanence. 

Technical tags, equipment serials, network statuses, and location badges utilize `label-technical` in uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.08em`), evoking calibrated instrument panels. Numbers in telemetry grids must utilize tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical alignment in telemetry tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system is constructed on a 12-column architectural grid on desktop (max content constraint `1280px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile.

Layout rules prioritize disciplined alignment and structural negative space:
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Generous outer margins of `3rem` (`space-desktop`), grid gutters of `1.5rem`. Editorial side-by-side splits with strict rectangular boundaries.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` margins, collapsing multi-column service matrices to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Spec tables reflow vertically; camera feeds take full-bleed width with hairline separating borders.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Major sections breathe with `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to `space-3xl` (`6rem`), avoiding cramped clusters and establishing industrial dignity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal stacking and low-contrast hairline outlines** rather than fuzzy dropshadows or glossy blurs.

- **Surface Tiers**: Base background uses `neutral` (`#F4F6F9`). Cards and elevation modules sit atop it in pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders. In dark mode consoles, surfaces stack from `#0F1A2C` (floor) to `#132238` (modules) delineated by `#1E304B` hairlines.
- **Precision Borders**: Every card, image frame, and input uses a strict 1px boundary. Interactive states replace the grey boundary with `#0097B2` or `#132238` without layout shift.
- **Minimal Ambient Shadow**: Standard components use zero drop shadow. Floating overlays, command modals, and active quotation drawers utilize a single, tight, non-diffuse industrial shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(19, 34, 56, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 1 (Soft / Engineered)** with a default corner radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`). 

Large containers, camera feed viewports, and cards stay nearly sharp (`4px` to `6px`), reinforcing the physical feel of extruded metal rack mounts, high-definition monitor bezels, and CCTV hardware housings. 

Pill shapes and bubbly radii are prohibited, with one precise exception: live camera status indicators and device counter badges, which use circular pills to signal optical lenses and status LEDs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#132238` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` corner radius. Subtle hover transitions to `#0F1A2C` with a 1px inner teal accent bar or `#0097B2` outline.
- **Accent Action Button**: Solid `#0097B2` background, white bold typography, ideal for emergency dispatch, quotation requests, and live system demos. Hover shifts to `#0EA5E9`.
- **Secondary / Technical Outline**: Pure white surface, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#132238` text. Hover adds 1px `#132238` border and slate tint background.
- **Sizing**: Default height 44px (field work touch targets), compact 36px for operational tables.

### Cards & Hardware Panels
- **Container Structure**: Clean white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border, `4px` corner radius. 
- **Image Integration**: Security equipment and installation photography are presented within aspect-ratio constrained, edge-to-edge or inset frames with a hairline inner border. No rounded circular cuts for camera hardware.
- **Technical Header**: Cards feature an upper eyebrow with `label-technical` formatting (e.g., `4K COLORVU IP SERİSİ | İVRİNDİ SAHA TESLİM`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#132238`, padding `10px 14px`, radius `4px`.
- **Focus State**: 1px solid `#0097B2` with a discrete `0 0 0 2px rgba(0, 151, 178, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square 18x18mm checkboxes with 2px radius. Check fill in `#0097B2`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Live Stream Badge**: Background `#0F1A2C`, text `#FFFFFF`, featuring a pulsing 6px `#10B981` dot.
- **Spec Chip**: Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `label-technical` in `#132238`.

### Tables & Specification Grids
- Tabular figures, alternating background bands (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`), divided by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Column headers in bold uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` at 12px with high contrast.

### System-Specific Component: Telemetry & Camera Monitor Tile
- Black/Navy (`#0F1A2C`) feed housing with live timestamp in monospace/Inter `11px`, channel identifier badge in the top-left corner, and connection latency indicator in the top-right corner.
